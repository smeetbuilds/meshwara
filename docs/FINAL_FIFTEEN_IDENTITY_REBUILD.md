## Fidelity and claims

The above are **object-specific artistic 3D illustrations** with material
separation and authored profile geometry. This gate is *not* evidence of:

- Ultra-photorealism, external reference shape accuracy, scanned textures
  or real product dimensions for these fifteen modules
- Impact or sports protective equipment certification
- Medical device, electrical, lifting or building safety certification
- Independent artist-approved views from three angles or real mobile GPU fps

Technical gates verify unique scene structures, required named assemblies,
TSX integrity, 500 static page generation and preview-to-download ZIP parity.
All 500 items retain a visual approval status of **pending**. The strict
visual-release check must remain red until a genuine human multi-angle
review supplies a defensible approval record.
